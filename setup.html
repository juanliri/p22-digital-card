Direct pass integration for iPhone Wallet and paired Apple Watch.

How Apple Watch Wallet Passes Work:

When an official .pkpass is installed into your iPhone's Apple Wallet, Apple Watch automatically mirrors it in the watch's Wallet app. Double-clicking the side button on your Apple Watch instantly opens the pass on your wrist.

Apple Cryptographic Signature Requirement:

Apple requires every .pkpass bundle to be cryptographically signed using an Apple Developer Pass Type ID certificate (pass.com.p22corp.card). Unsigned passes are blocked by iOS for security.
