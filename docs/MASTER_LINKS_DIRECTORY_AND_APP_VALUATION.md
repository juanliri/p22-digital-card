## PART 2: FULL STACK OVERVIEW, PERKS & SYSTEM POTENTIAL

Our hybrid architecture combines edge compute with zero-maintenance serverless pipelines, eliminating costly SaaS subscriptions ($0 monthly recurring software fees).

```mermaid
graph TD
    Client[Client / Contracting Officer] -->|Tap NFC / Scan QR| Edge[Vercel Edge Network - sub-40ms]
    Edge --> PublicCard[Public Digital Card - 5 Personas]
    Edge --> StaffBadge[PWA Staff Badge - 100dvh Offline]
    PublicCard --> VCF[vCard 3.0 with Base64 Portrait]
    PublicCard --> Wallet[Apple Wallet .pkpass / Google Pay JWT]
    PublicCard --> OCR[On-Device Tesseract + Cloud AI OCR]
    StaffBadge --> OCR
    OCR --> ServerlessAPI[/api/lead & /api/track]
    ServerlessAPI --> GoogleSheets[(Google Sheets Real-Time DB - Leads_Vault & Dashboard)]
```

### Key Stack Superpowers & Perks:

1. **Zero Recurring SaaS Subscriptions ($0/mo Overhead)**:
   - Competitive platforms like Popl, Blinq, HiHello, or Linq charge **$8 to $25 per user per month** plus enterprise add-on fees for team management, CRM sync, and custom domains ($1,500 - $3,000/yr).
   - Our system runs 100% on standard Vercel serverless + Google Cloud free tier. Zero monthly fees.

2. **Defense Contractor Language & Identity Lockdown**:
   - Hardened with verified CAGE Code `169D8`, UEI `X3HUQZ66P6N3`, and FAR/DFARS compliance tokens.
   - Tailored specifically for federal procurement officers who demand formal past performance verification.

3. **100% Offline Expo Floor Failsafe**:
   - If convention center Wi-Fi drops completely:
     - The Service Worker (`sw.js`) serves cached badge screens.
     - The 9:16 Offline Lock Screen wallpaper allows phone-to-phone QR scanning with the phone completely locked.
     - The on-device Tesseract OCR engine transcribes client cards locally on the phone's CPU with zero internet connection.
     - Leads are cached in local browser storage and batch-synced to Google Sheets as soon as cellular signal reconnects.

4. **Bi-Directional Google Sheets Integration**:
   - No expensive Salesforce or HubSpot seats required for expo attendees.
   - Non-technical executives can open their standard Google Sheets app on their iPad or laptop to see leads, names, phone numbers, and OCR notes populating in real time as staff talk to visitors at the booth.
